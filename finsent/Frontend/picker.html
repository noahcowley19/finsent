Mark Minervini's trend template is a systematic approach to identifying stocks in strong uptrends with institutional support. To qualify, a stock must meet all of the following criteria:

1. Price Above Moving Averages

Current price > 150-day MA > 200-day MA, indicating established uptrend.

2. Rising 150-Day MA

150-day MA is trending up over the past month, showing sustained momentum.

3. Stable 200-Day MA

200-day MA is flat or rising for at least 1 month, confirming trend stability.

4. Short-Term Strength

50-day MA > 150-day MA > 200-day MA, showing acceleration in the trend.

5. Above 50-Day MA

Current price > 50-day MA, indicating short-term uptrend continuation.

6. Strong Recovery

Price is at least 30% above its 52-week low, showing significant recovery.

7. Near 52-Week High

Price is within 25% of its 52-week high, demonstrating leadership.

8. Relative Strength ≥ 70

RS Rating of 70+ indicates outperformance vs. the S&P 500.

Note: This screener only includes stocks that meet ALL 8 criteria. The methodology focuses on growth momentum stocks with established uptrends and institutional support. Results are most effective when combined with proper risk management and position sizing.
